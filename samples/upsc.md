| `mtz-bench-upsc-001` | Indian Polity — No-Confidence Motion vs Censure Motion (Drill Variant 1) | [Prelims Concept Check #1] With reference to parliamentary motions in India, which statement accurately distinguishes a No-Confidence Motion from a Censure Motion? | **B** | **A** — Individual Minister & Article 75 Textual Trap: Collective responsibility under Art. 75(3) is to the House of the People (Lok Sabha) as a whole; Rule 198 of Lok Sabha Rules (not Art. 75 text) governs No-Confidence Motions, and they apply only to the entire Council, never one minister. | No-Confidence = Lok Sabha ONLY + Entire Council + No reason needed + Rule 198 (phrase absent in Constitution text). |
| `mtz-bench-upsc-002` | Indian Economy — RBI Open Market Operations & Liquidity (Drill Variant 1) | [Prelims Concept Check #2] When the Reserve Bank of India conducts an outright SALE of Government Securities (G-Secs) under Open Market Operations (OMO), what is the immediate monetary effect? | **A** | **B** — Buyer-vs-Seller Cash-Flow Direction Reversal Trap: Students see "Sale of G-Secs" and think money enters the market. | `mtz-bench-upsc-003` | Environment & Ecology — Biosphere Reserves vs National Parks (Drill Variant 1) | [Prelims Concept Check #3] Which legal distinction between a National Park and a Wildlife Sanctuary under the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 is accurate? | **C** | **A** — Species-Specificity & Grazing Permission Reversal Trap: Both National Parks and Sanctuaries protect habitats/ecosystems (neither is restricted by law to only a single species), and National Parks carry the stricter statutory prohibition on grazing. | National Park = ZERO grazing + stricter rights extinguishment; Sanctuary = grazing regulable by Chief Wildlife Warden. |
